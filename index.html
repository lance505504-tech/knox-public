Get started in 60 seconds

Here's exactly how it works.

Knox doesn't connect to your AI directly. It generates text that you paste. That's what makes it work with any AI — no integration, no plugin, no API key.

1
Open Knox and set up your first project
Open Knox Easy or Knox Credential. Enter your name — you're straight in. Add a project: give it a name, a description, and a task or two. Takes two minutes the first time.
2
Go to the Briefing tab
Type what you want to work on today in the Session Focus field. Select which projects to include. Click Generate. Knox produces a structured briefing — your projects, open tasks, recent decisions, rules, and context all in one block of text.
3
Copy the briefing — then copy the opening instruction
Two Copy buttons in the Briefing tab. Copy the briefing first. Then copy the Session Opening Instruction below it — this tells your AI to confirm what it read, ask one question, and wait before doing anything.
4
Open a new AI conversation and paste both
Open Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot — whichever you use. Paste the briefing as your first message. Paste the opening instruction immediately after. Send. Your AI now knows your projects, your history, your rules, and what you want to work on today.
Your AI will respond by confirming what it understood, asking one focused question, then waiting. Answer it — then say go. Work begins from a standing start, not a standing explanation.
5
At the end of the session — log what was decided
Go back to Knox. In your project, add a Decision for anything important that was agreed, and tick off any tasks that got done. Next session, the briefing will include those decisions automatically — your AI never re-derives what you've already settled.
6
That's the loop
Brief → work → log → brief. Each session starts sharper than the last. Your AI builds on what was decided rather than re-deriving it. Over time Knox becomes the institutional memory of everything you've built with AI — decisions, context, and what happens next.

FAQ

Questions people ask
before they try it.

What does Knox actually do? +
Knox gives your AI assistant a memory. Before each session you generate a briefing — a structured summary of your projects, decisions, rules, and context — and paste it in. Your AI reads it and picks up where you left off. The simplest way to find out if it works for you is to try it. Set up one project, generate a briefing, paste it into your next AI session, and see if the conversation feels different. Most people notice immediately.
Will Knox solve all my AI memory problems? +
Knox solves the briefing problem — your AI starts each session knowing your context. But AI assistants don't retain files between sessions, even in features like "Projects." For that you need a cloud drive or repository. The combination that works best is Knox for context and briefing, plus Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or a GitHub repo for actual files — and telling your AI where to find them. Knox Credential lets you store those service details so your AI knows exactly where to look.
Is this really free? +
Yes. Knox Easy and Knox Credential are both free to download and use. No subscription, no trial period, no credit card. Licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0 — free for personal and team use. The only restriction is you can't sell it or incorporate it into a commercial product without permission.
Do I need to create an account? +
No. Knox runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no servers, no sign-up. Open the file, enter your name, you're in.
Where is my data stored? +
In your browser's localStorage — a private storage area on your device that no external service can access. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data, Knox data clears too — so export a backup regularly.
What's the difference between Knox Easy and Knox Credential? +
Knox Easy is session memory — projects, decisions, rules, briefing generator. It opens instantly with no setup. Knox Credential adds a full credential vault for storing API keys, service logins, and account details for services like Google Drive, GitHub, Supabase, and anything else you use. Start with Easy. Move to Credential if you need the vault.
Does it work with ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot — not just Claude? +
Yes. Knox generates plain text. Any AI assistant that can read a message can use a Knox briefing. No integration, no plugin, no API key required. Switch between AI assistants freely — your briefing works with all of them.
What if I forget my PIN? +
Knox Easy: go to Settings and remove the PIN — opens without one. Knox Credential: use the PIN reset flow which sends a code to your registered email. Either way your data is safe — the PIN protects access, it doesn't encrypt the data.
Can I share my vault with my team or family? +
Yes. Export your vault as a JSON file and share it. The recipient imports it into their own Knox. Share the file via a trusted channel like Signal or WhatsApp rather than unprotected email — it contains your project data and, if using Knox Credential, your credentials.
Can I self-host Knox? +
Yes. Fork the repo on GitHub, deploy to Render, Netlify, Vercel, or any static host. No build step, no server required — just point it at the HTML file. Full instructions in the README.

How Knox was built

We built this tool badly.
That's why it exists.

Knox was built in a single long AI session — on the fly, without proper structure, without following the rules it was designed to enforce.

The session started with a simple question: "How do we fix the context and memory problem with AI?" Rather than thinking it through first, we went straight into building. The result was a longer road than it needed to be — multiple rebuilds, unnecessary steps, scope that crept in without being declared, decisions made mid-build that should have been made before a line of code was written.

At the end of the session, the question was asked: "Did you build this following the rules? Could you have built it better if you had?" The honest answer was no, and yes.

"The irony isn't lost on me — we spent a session building a tool specifically to impose structure on AI sessions, while running an unstructured AI session to build it."

— Claude, on reviewing the session

Knox is not a final solution. It is a meaningful step in the right direction — a practical tool that imposes just enough structure to prevent the worst of the drift, the rebuilding, and the arguments that come from AI sessions that start without a clear brief and rules that stick.

If you've ever had a long AI session that went off track, produced something you didn't quite want, or required you to explain the same thing three times — Knox is the tool we wish we'd had before we started building Knox.
